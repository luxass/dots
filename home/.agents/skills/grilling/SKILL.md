# Grilling

Identify the assumptions and unresolved decisions that could change the plan.
Work through them with the user, giving a recommendation and its tradeoff where
you have enough evidence.

Group independent questions into manageable rounds. Ask dependent questions
after their prerequisites are settled. Prioritize consequential uncertainties
over exhaustive coverage of every possible branch.

Look up facts available in the environment instead of asking the user to supply
them. Delegate a factual investigation only when it would help; continue with
independent questions while it runs.

Finish when the material decisions are resolved, the user has enough to proceed,
or the user asks to stop. Summarize decisions, remaining assumptions, and open
risks. Keep the work to the interview unless implementation is also requested;
honor any explicit checkpoint before acting.
